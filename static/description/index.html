Website Booking System

Let your customers book any product by date, time slot and plan straight from your Odoo website, with live availability, per-plan capacity and the standard checkout.

Odoo 19.0Date & calendar bookingTime slotsPlans & pricesLive capacity

Overview

Courts, rooms, tours, movie shows, workshops, studio sessions: anything sold for a given day and time. Create your plans (Silver, Gold, Platinum...) and time slots (09:00 - 11:00, 12:00 - 14:00...) once, then decide for each product which weekdays are open, which dates are closed, how many seats each plan has and what each plan costs.

On the product page a Book Now popup walks the customer through four quick steps: pick a date (date strip or calendar), choose a time, choose a plan and set the number of guests. The booking goes through the normal Odoo website cart and payment, and a Booking Order is created automatically once the sale order is confirmed.

Date strip + calendar

Pick a date from a scrollable strip or open a month calendar.

Time slots & plans

First available slot and plan are preselected, one click to change.

Live capacity

Each plan shows how many are left. Sold-out options are disabled.

Standard checkout

Cart, payment and sale order stay 100% native Odoo.

Key Features

Everything a booking website needs, nothing it does not

Date strip and calendar picker

Customers choose a date from a scrollable date strip or from a month calendar. Closed dates are disabled and tagged Closed, so nobody wastes time on a date that cannot be booked.

Shared time slots

Define slots such as 09:00 - 11:00 once in Configuration > Time Slots and reuse them on every product. Five default slots are installed for you.

Plans with their own price

Create plans once (Silver, Gold, Platinum...). On each product choose which plans are offered and set the price of each plan for that product. Plan names and descriptions are translatable.

Weekday configuration per product

Open the weekdays you need; every other weekday is closed. Each open day can offer all time slots or only a selection of them (for example a late show only on Friday and Saturday).

Closing periods

Add date ranges for holidays, maintenance or private events and attach them to any product. Those dates are disabled in both the date strip and the calendar.

Booking window and advance limit

Set a start and end date for each product, and optionally limit advance booking to a number of days (for example 7 days ahead). Slots that already started today are hidden.

Capacity per plan, date and slot

Max Booking Qty applies to every plan on every date and time slot, so Gold can be full while Silver still has seats. The popup shows the quantity left.

Guests and live total

A simple stepper limits guests to what is left for the chosen plan, and the total updates instantly with the plan price.

No double booking

The selection is re-checked when the customer pays and again when the order is confirmed. Confirmation takes a database lock per product, so two customers paying for the last seat at the same moment cannot both get it.

Automatic booking orders

When the sale order is confirmed a Booking Order (numbered BK/2026/00001) is created for every booked line. Mark it Done after the visit, or Cancel it.

Cancel releases the seats

Cancelling a booking order frees its quantity immediately. Setting a cancelled booking back to Confirmed is only possible while the quantity is still available.

Reporting

A Check Booking Quantity button on the product opens a pivot by date, time slot and plan. The Reporting menu offers pivot and graph views for all bookings.

Native website checkout

Booking products use the standard Odoo cart, payment and sale order. Add to Cart and Buy Now are hidden on booking products, so a booking can never skip the date and time selection.

Theme friendly and light

The popup follows the colour of your website theme, adapts to mobile screens and uses plain JavaScript without extra libraries.

How It Works

1

Configure once

Create your plans and time slots in Bookings > Configuration. Add closing periods you want to reuse.

2

Set up a product

Tick Available for Booking, open the Booking & Reservation tab and fill in dates, weekdays, plan prices and quantity. Publish the product.

3

Customer books

On the product page the customer clicks Book Now, picks date, time, plan and guests, then checks out as usual.

4

You manage

A Booking Order appears when the order is confirmed. Follow it in the list, pivot or graph, and mark it Done or Cancel it.

Four steps, one screen

Frequently Asked Questions

Do customers need an account to book?

A booking goes through the normal website cart and checkout, so it follows your shop settings for guest checkout and sign-in.

What happens when two customers want the last seat?

The selection is validated again at payment and at order confirmation, and confirmation locks the product while it checks. The second customer gets a clear message telling how many are left.

Can the same plan have a different price on each product?

Yes. Plans are shared, but the price of a plan is set on each product.

Are pricelists applied?

No. The price of the chosen plan on the product is used as the unit price of the order line.

Does cancelling a booking order refund the customer?

No. Cancelling a booking order only releases the quantity on the date and time slot. Cancel the sale order or issue the refund with your usual Odoo process.

How far ahead can customers book?

Up to the product end date, or the advance limit if you set one. Without either, the next 90 days are offered.

Which timezone is used for today and for slots that already started?

The timezone of the user or website visitor making the request. Slot times are compared with that clock.

Can a weekday offer only some time slots?

Yes. In Configure Day Slots add a line for the weekday and choose its time slots. Leave the field empty to offer all active time slots.
